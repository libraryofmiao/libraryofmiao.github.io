Genesis of NALC Miao

From a local challenge to a new learning model

The original NALC emerged from an effort to respond to educational and youth-development challenges in Miao and the remote Vijaynagar circle.

The Problem Identified · 2021

Taking charge as Additional Deputy Commissioner (ADC) of Miao in February 2021, Singh observed severe educational bottlenecks across Miao and the cutoff Vijaynagar circle—high secondary dropout rates, poor pass percentages, and rising youth substance abuse, paired with an absence of functional libraries or exam preparation facilities.

Architectural & Pedagogical Shift

Drawing on his engineering background, he avoided traditional sterile government reading rooms. Instead, he conceptualized NALC as a free, ergonomic, cafe-style hub featuring modular light wood furnishings, beanbags, sound-insulated competitive study pods, high-speed Wi-Fi, tablets, and curated book collections for all age brackets.

Convergence Model

He integrated local schools within 500 meters so their mandatory library periods were conducted inside NALC, instituted weekly documentary screenings, and organized mentorship sessions with civil servants and subject experts.

National Honor

NALC was recognized through the Prime Minister’s Award for Excellence in Public Administration (Innovation – District category), conferred on Civil Services Day in April 2023 by Prime Minister Narendra Modi.

Institutional Imperatives

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To institutionalize a decentralized network of smart, futuristic learning spaces across remote regions, transforming traditional public libraries into hubs of digital literacy, intellectual community, and equal opportunity for every aspiring student.

Our Mission

  • Democratize Access: Provide state-of-the-art educational infrastructure and high-speed resources completely free of cost to vulnerable sections.
  • Foster Learning Culture: Shift the paradigm from rigid libraries to a vibrant “leisure-learning” ecosystem that inspires curiosity.
  • Bridge the Digital Divide: Integrate emerging ed-tech tools, tablets, and localized resources to equip rural students on par with urban peers.
