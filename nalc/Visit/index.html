A record of distinguished visits to NALC Miao.

This page presents a chronological record of visits by senior government officers, public leaders, IAS trainees and other distinguished stakeholders associated with the New Age Learning Centre at the Sub Divisional Library, Miao.

2022
30 November 2022

Visit of Seven IAS Trainees from LBSNAA

Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration (LBSNAA)

Seven IAS trainees from the Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration (LBSNAA) visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 30 November 2022.

18 December 2022

Visit of Shri Sanjay Kothari

Retired IAS Officer · Former Chief Vigilance Commissioner, Government of India

Shri Sanjay Kothari, Retired IAS Officer and former Chief Vigilance Commissioner, Government of India, visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 18 December 2022.

2023
15 January 2023

Visit of the Chief Secretary

Shri Dharmendra · Chief Secretary, Government of Arunachal Pradesh

Shri Dharmendra, Chief Secretary, Government of Arunachal Pradesh, visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 15 January 2023.

14 August 2023

Visit of Hon'ble Home Minister Chowna Mein and Shri Kamlung Mossang

Hon'ble Home Minister Chowna Mein · Shri Kamlung Mossang, Minister

Hon'ble Home Minister Chowna Mein, accompanied by Minister Shri Kamlung Mossang, visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 14 August 2023.

30 September 2023

Visit of IAS Trainees from LBSNAA

2023 Batch · Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration

IAS trainees of the 2023 batch from the Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration (LBSNAA) visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 30 September 2023.

30 December 2023

Visit of Smt. Anagha Parnaik

First Lady of Arunachal Pradesh

Smt. Anagha Parnaik, First Lady of Arunachal Pradesh, visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 30 December 2023.

30 January 2024

Visit of IAS Trainees During the Bharat Darshan Programme

2023 Batch · Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration

IAS trainees of the 2023 batch from the Lal Bahadur Shastri National Academy of Administration (LBSNAA) visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, during the Bharat Darshan Programme on 30 January 2024.

4 March 2024

Farewell Visit of the Deputy Commissioner, Changlang

Deputy Commissioner, Changlang District

The Deputy Commissioner of Changlang visited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 4 March 2024, on the occasion of a farewell.

Shri Sunny K. Singh, IAS, revisited the New Age Learning Centre, Miao, on 17 February 2026.
