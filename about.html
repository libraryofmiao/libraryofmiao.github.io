About the Sub Divisional Library

Welcome to the Subdivisional Library, Miao

Discover a world of knowledge in the heart of Changlang district, Arunachal Pradesh. Nestled along the scenic Noa-Dehing River near Namdapha National Park, our library was established to bridge the educational gap in this beautiful, mountainous terrain. For decades, we have served as a vital cultural and intellectual sanctuary for the diverse indigenous communities and respected citizens of the eastern Himalayas.

Connecting Communities, Empowering Minds

We reach out to students, research scholars, and local residents across remote villages, providing a quiet space for lifelong learning.

Serving the community for over four decades

The Sub Divisional Library Miao is a government public library located in Miao, the headquarters of Miao sub-division in Changlang district, Arunachal Pradesh. Situated in one of India’s easternmost regions, near the Assam border and close to Namdapha National Park, the library has long served students, readers, and residents of the sub-division.

Public libraries in Arunachal Pradesh function under the state system of library services. At the sub-divisional level, these institutions act as important centres for reading, study, and access to books for people living in both the town and the surrounding villages.

Key milestones

1983

Established as Block Library

The library was first established as a Block Library on 19 April 1983. At that time it served the local population of the Miao block, providing basic reading and reference facilities.

2000

Upgraded to Sub Divisional Library

On 20 October 2000, the institution was formally upgraded from Block Library to Sub Divisional Library. This elevation expanded its administrative status and its role as a central library facility for the entire Miao sub-division.

2021

Foundation stone of the new building

The foundation stone of the new library building was laid on 7 June 2021. This marked an important step towards providing a permanent and improved physical infrastructure for the Sub Divisional Library.

The present building

Today the Sub Divisional Library continues to serve the community from its current premises in Miao.

Location and role

Miao is a notified town and the administrative headquarters of Miao Circle and Miao sub-division in Changlang district. The sub-division covers both the town and a large number of surrounding villages. The library is intended to serve readers from this wider area.

As a Sub Divisional Library, it forms part of the hierarchical public library system of Arunachal Pradesh, standing above village and block-level reading rooms and below the District Library. Its traditional functions include maintaining a collection of books for general reading, supporting students, and providing a quiet space for study.
