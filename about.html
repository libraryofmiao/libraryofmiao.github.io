About the Sub Divisional Library Miao

A government public library reimagined as a New Age Learning Centre — bringing books, technology, opportunity and a culture of learning together in Miao.

Our Story

A paradise for every
book lover

The Sub Divisional Library Miao — reimagined as the New Age Learning Centre (NALC) — is the brainchild of Miao Additional Deputy Commissioner Shri Sunny K. Singh. It is Arunachal Pradesh's first futuristic public library, housed in a two-storey building spanning 2,000 sq ft.

Set up at an estimated cost of ₹30 Lakh+ through a combination of community contributions and government support, the air-conditioned facility is fully equipped with tablets, computers, e-readers, a virtual reality headset and free Wi-Fi.

NALC serves as an interactive learning hub for local schools and students, encouraging dedicated library and reading periods. It also supports educational initiatives such as OIL Super 30 residential classes for JEE and NEET aspirants and Project Safal, which works to improve board-examination outcomes and has earned recognition from the PMO.

The Concept

More than a library

Its purpose is not only to provide access to books, but to help children, teenagers, competitive-exam aspirants, students and lifelong learners discover knowledge and build confidence.

History

Serving the community for over four decades

The Sub Divisional Library Miao is a government public library located in Miao, the headquarters of Miao sub-division in Changlang district, Arunachal Pradesh. From its origins as a Block Library, the institution has evolved into a modern learning environment while continuing its public-library role.

1983

Established as Block Library

The library was first established as a Block Library on 19 April 1983, serving the local population of the Miao block with reading and reference facilities.

2000

Upgraded to Sub Divisional Library

On 20 October 2000, the institution was formally upgraded from Block Library to Sub Divisional Library, expanding its role as a central library facility for Miao sub-division.

2021

Foundation stone of the new building

The foundation stone of the new library building was laid on 7 June 2021, marking an important step towards improved infrastructure for the library.

2022

New Age Learning Centre inaugurated

The reimagined library was inaugurated as the New Age Learning Centre (NALC) on 7 May 2022, introducing a modern, technology-enabled approach to public learning.

Community & Learning

Connecting communities, empowering minds

NALC provides a welcoming environment for students, readers and residents. Through school engagement, reading periods, academic support, competitive-exam preparation and access to modern digital resources, the centre aims to make learning more accessible and meaningful.

A library is not simply a place to keep books. It is a place where curiosity can grow, ideas can meet, and a learner can begin to imagine a larger future.
The Library Today

A modern public learning space

Today, the Sub Divisional Library Miao continues to serve the people of Miao and the wider sub-division. Its physical and digital resources support reading, study, academic preparation and technology-enabled learning, while the NALC concept expands the library's role as a community learning centre.
