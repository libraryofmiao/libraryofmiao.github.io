Our History & Identity

About the Sub Divisional Library

A government public library serving readers, students and the wider community of Miao Sub-Division in Changlang District, Arunachal Pradesh.

A library with a long-standing public purpose

The Sub Divisional Library Miao is a government public library located at Miao, the headquarters of Miao Sub-Division in Changlang District, Arunachal Pradesh. Since its establishment as a Block Library in 1983, it has remained a local place for reading, study, reference and access to knowledge.

Its role has grown with the community. Today, the library serves students, readers and residents of Miao and the surrounding area, while continuing the basic public-library purpose of making books, information and a suitable reading environment accessible to the community.

“A library is more than a collection of books. It is a shared space for learning, curiosity, reflection and opportunity.”

From a block library to a sub-divisional institution

The history of the library reflects the development of public library services in Miao. Its formal milestones mark the transition from a block-level facility to the present Sub Divisional Library and the continuing effort to provide better infrastructure for readers.

1983

Established as Block Library

The library was established as a Block Library on 19 April 1983, providing reading and reference facilities for the local community.

2000

Upgraded to Sub Divisional Library

On 20 October 2000, the institution was upgraded from Block Library to Sub Divisional Library, reflecting its wider role in serving the Miao Sub-Division.

2021

Foundation stone of the new building

The foundation stone of the new library building was laid on 7 June 2021, marking an important step towards improved library infrastructure.

The library today

The Sub Divisional Library continues to serve as a public learning and reading space in Miao. Its purpose remains straightforward and enduring: to support reading, study, access to information and the development of a stronger culture of learning within the community.

The library also provides a foundation for newer initiatives that connect traditional library services with the changing needs of children, young people, students and other learners.
