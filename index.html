About the Library

More than a library.

Through quizzes, storytelling, public speaking, photography, painting, music, yoga, career guidance and other activity-based programmes, NALC encourages young minds to step beyond textbooks, discover their talents and develop their personalities.

Because the goal isn't simply to produce better readers or better exam scores. The goal is to help create confident, curious and capable young people who dare to dream bigger.

NALC Miao isn't just a library. It's a place where curiosity gets a chance to become a future. 🚀
