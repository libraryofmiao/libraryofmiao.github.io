Welcome to the Subdivisional Library, Miao

Discover a world of knowledge in the heart of Changlang district, Arunachal Pradesh. Nestled along the scenic Noa-Dehing River near Namdapha National Park, our library was established to bridge the educational gap in this beautiful, mountainous terrain. For decades, we have served as a vital cultural and intellectual sanctuary for the diverse indigenous communities and respected citizens of the eastern Himalayas.

Connecting Communities, Empowering Minds

We reach out to students, research scholars, and local residents across remote villages, providing a quiet space for lifelong learning.

Step Inside: An Appeal to Our Community

"A library thrives on the curiosity of its people. This space belongs to you—the students, the dreamers, the elders, and the children of Miao. We warmly invite you to step through our doors, explore our shelves, and make reading a daily habit. Let us learn, grow, and build a brighter future together."
