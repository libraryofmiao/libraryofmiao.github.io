Our Story

A paradise for every
book lover

The Sub Divisional Library Miao — reimagined as the New Age Learning Centre (NALC) — is the brainchild of Miao Additional Deputy Commissioner Shri Sunny K. Singh. It is Arunachal Pradesh's first futuristic public library, housed in a two-storey building spanning 2,000 sq ft.

Set up at an estimated cost of ₹30 Lakh+ through a combination of community contributions and government support, the air-conditioned facility is fully equipped with tablets, computers, e-readers, virtual reality headset, and free Wi-Fi. It serves as an interactive hub for local schools and students, hosting dedicated library and reading periods at the NALC premises.

Under its CSR initiative, Oil India Limited (OIL) runs Super 30 residential classes for JEE and NEET aspirants here. The NALC also drives Project Safal — a district administration initiative to improve board exam passing percentages. It has earned recognition from the Prime Minister's Office.

What We Offer

Our Services & Resources

Diverse Book Collection

Fiction, non-fiction, children's books, biographies, autobiographies, motivational titles, and academic books across all genres.

Smart Learning Screens

State-of-the-art smart screens and digital tablets for interactive learning, e-books, and multimedia educational content.

Free High-Speed Wi-Fi

Complimentary high-speed internet access for all members to support research, online learning, and digital exploration.

Competitive Exam Resources

Dedicated resources for UPSC Civil Services, NDA, JEE, NEET preparation — including Super 30 classes by Oil India Limited.

School Central Library

Acts as the central library for all schools within 500 metres. Structured library periods are held at NALC for students.

Project Safal

Supporting the district administration's Project Safal initiative to improve board exam results for children across the region.
